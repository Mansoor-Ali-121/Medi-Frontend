Scope of Platform Role

ShifaCare Plus is a digital healthcare coordination platform. We verify and connect patients with independent Pakistan Medical and Dental Council (PMDC) registered physicians and Pakistan Nursing Council (PNC) certified home nurses.

While we rigorously vet qualifications, valid credentials, and criminal background records, the actual medical treatment, diagnosis, surgical interventions, and direct clinical decisions are made solely by the treating licensed healthcare professionals in their independent professional capacity.

06

Prescription & Injectable Medication Safety

PNC Mandatory Medication Requirement: Attending nurses are legally prohibited under Pakistan Nursing Council regulations from administering intravenous (IV) or intramuscular (IM) injectables, controlled narcotics, or chemotherapy drugs without a stamped, dated, written prescription issued by a PMDC-licensed physician. Families must supply sealed original pharmaceuticals purchased from registered pharmacies.
07

Safe Home Working Environment & Family Conduct

Families contracting home care nursing must ensure a secure, respectful, and dignified environment:

  • Zero Harassment Policy: Any verbal abuse, physical intimidation, or harassment towards female or male nursing staff results in immediate termination of service, forfeiture of deposits, and reporting to ICT Police.
  • Sanitary Accommodation: For 24/7 resident nurses, families must provide a private, clean resting room with bathroom facilities and hygienic meals.
  • Domestic Scope Limitation: Nurses are clinical healthcare practitioners; they must not be assigned general household domestic cleaning, cooking, or non-medical family chores.
