Every nurse registered on ShifaCare Plus undergoes a rigorous 3-tier credentialing protocol before attending patients:

  • PNC License Verification: Real-time cross-check with the Pakistan Nursing Council registry (BScN / Midwifery registration numbers).
  • Police Background Clearance: Character and security verification through Islamabad Capital Police and Punjab Police Citizen Facilitation Centers.
  • Clinical Skills Validation: Practical testing in cannula insertion, IV infusion, tracheostomy hygiene, Ryle's tube feeding, and emergency CPR.

Families can inspect the caregiver's original PNC registration card and digital profile badge upon arrival at their home.

24-Hour Live-In Nursing

Around-the-clock dedicated clinical support with scheduled rotation. Best suited for stroke rehabilitation, coma, and ventilator support.
