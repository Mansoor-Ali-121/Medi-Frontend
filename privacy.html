01

Introduction & Regulatory Scope

Welcome to ShifaCare Plus ("we", "our", or "the Portal"), an integrated healthcare coordination platform serving the twin cities of Islamabad and Rawalpindi, Pakistan. This Privacy Policy governs our collection, storage, processing, and disclosure of Personal Identifiable Information (PII) and Protected Health Information (PHI) obtained via our web portal, mobile interfaces, telephone booking desks, and clinical home visits.

Applicable Statutory & Regulatory Framework: Our digital practices operate in strict alignment with the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) 2016, the Pakistan Medical and Dental Council (PMDC) Code of Ethics, the Pakistan Nursing Council (PNC) Practice Acts, and international HIPAA-comparable electronic health record confidentiality principles.
02

Information We Collect

To facilitate safe clinical appointments, emergency home nursing dispatch, and verified hospital admissions, we collect the following categories of information:

Personal Identification Data
  • Full legal name, CNIC number (for identity verification)
  • Residential physical address, sector, or mohalla
  • Active mobile phone number & WhatsApp contact
  • Emergency contact person & relationship
Clinical & Medical Health Records (PHI)
  • Past medical history, surgical summaries, and chronic illnesses
  • Known drug allergies and current pharmacotherapy
  • Diagnostic lab tests, radiological imaging, and doctor prescriptions
  • Daily vitals logs: Blood Pressure, SpO2, Pulse, Blood Glucose
03

Purpose of Data Processing

We process health information exclusively for legitimate medical care coordination and portal administration:

  • Clinical Scheduling: Transmitting essential appointment summaries to licensed medical consultants at partner hospitals.
  • Home Nursing Briefings: Providing PNC-registered nurses with pre-visit vitals baselines and doctor treatment directives before arriving at patient homes in Islamabad/Rawalpindi.
  • SMS & WhatsApp Notifications: Dispatching automated booking confirmations, tele-consultation links, nurse arrival alerts, and medicine intake reminders.
04

Home Nursing Care & In-Home Shift Record Logging

When booking 12-hour day/night shifts or 24/7 resident nursing care, attending nurses must log patient vital signs, wound dressing assessments, IV catheter flushes, and medication administrations into our secure portal dashboard.

Household Confidentiality Guarantee: All ShifaCare Plus nurses execute non-disclosure agreements regarding household privacy, family lifestyle, and domestic affairs observed during home shifts. Unlawful recording or photography inside patient residences without explicit written consent is strictly prohibited and constitutes grounds for immediate PNC license reporting and legal action.
05

Hospital & Consultant Integration

When you book appointments at affiliated tertiary centers (including Shifa International Hospital H-8, PIMS Islamabad G-8, Maroof International Hospital F-10, Holy Family Hospital, and CMH Rawalpindi), your clinical records are shared strictly under the principle of minimum necessary disclosure.

Hospitals process your records according to their own internal medical board charters and PMDC regulations. ShifaCare Plus does not maintain custody of hospital inpatient surgical theaters or clinical chart rooms outside the portal booking synchronization scope.

06

Emergency Rescue 1122 & Critical Disclosures

In life-threatening circumstances where a patient experiences acute cardiovascular collapse, severe respiratory distress, stroke symptoms, or severe trauma during a home nursing visit:

Emergency Life-Saving Override: Our clinical coordinator or assigned home nurse is authorized to disclose the patient's physical residence address, current vitals, known allergies, and critical health history directly to Punjab Emergency Service Rescue 1122, Islamabad Capital Territory EMS, or the nearest tertiary Emergency Room without prior patient signature.
07

Payment & Billing Security

ShifaCare Plus facilitates payments through State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) licensed payment gateways (including JazzCash, EasyPaisa, 1Link, and certified debit/credit card processors).

  • We never store raw credit card CVV codes or bank PINs on our servers.
  • Transactions are processed via PCI-DSS compliant tokenized payment channels.
  • Invoices and receipts are preserved in your Patient Portal for insurance reimbursement and tax compliance.
08

Data Security & Technical Safeguards

We employ robust administrative, physical, and electronic security safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized access, tampering, or loss of medical records:

TLS 1.3 Transmission

All network data is encrypted in transit between your device and our servers.

AES-256 at Rest

Database fields containing medical diagnoses are encrypted at rest.

Role-Based Access

Only your designated doctor and assigned nurse can inspect your active medical chart.

09

Data Retention Schedule

Clinical records must be maintained in accordance with PMDC medico-legal guidelines. We adhere to the following retention periods:

Pediatric Care & Neonatal Logs Until patient reaches age 25 Pediatric developmental liability statutes

10

Patient Rights & Health Record Portability

As a patient registered on ShifaCare Plus, you are endowed with distinct rights over your health records:

Right of Access & Portability

You may inspect or download your complete appointment history, lab summaries, and vitals charts in standardized PDF format via the Patient Dashboard.

Right to Rectification

If your demographic information, emergency contacts, or allergy notations are inaccurate, you may submit immediate correction requests.

Right to Erasure

You may request the deletion of your online portal account, subject to mandatory PMDC statutory healthcare retention laws.

Restriction of Processing

You may opt out of non-essential promotional communications, feedback surveys, or general healthcare newsletters at any time.

11

Cookies & Telemetry

We utilize strictly necessary technical session cookies to authenticate patient logins, remember filter preferences (such as Islamabad vs. Rawalpindi hospital searches), and safeguard against cross-site request forgery. We do not use persistent commercial ad trackers or sell data to third-party ad networks.
